{# ============================================================ features #}
Everything in one place

From the first scan to the final bill

One system for your guests, your kitchen, your waiters and your cash counter. It runs in the browser, on the phones and tablets you already have.

A QR menu on every table

Each table gets its own secure QR code. Guests see your live menu with photos and veg / non-veg marks.

Ordering without an app

Guests add dishes, place the order and order again for the next round, straight from the phone browser.

A live kitchen board

New KOTs appear on the kitchen screen by themselves. One tap moves them to Preparing, then Ready.

Waiters in the loop

Waiters see what is ready to serve, and can take the order on their phone for guests who prefer to ask.

GST bills in seconds

One bill from every round, with discount, CGST + SGST and round off. Prints on 80 mm thermal printers.

Split payments

Cash, card, UPI or online, in any mix on one bill. The change to return is shown on screen.

{# ======================================================== how it works #}
How it works

Four steps. No paper slips.

  1. 1

    Scan

    The guest scans the table's QR code with the phone camera or Google Lens.

  2. 2

    Order

    They pick dishes, add a note like "less spicy" and place the order. No login.

  3. 3

    Cook

    The KOT appears in the kitchen. The guest watches the status change live.

  4. 4

    Pay

    The cashier makes one GST bill from all rounds and records the payment. The table is free again.

{# ================================================================ FAQ #}
Questions

Good to know

Do my guests need to install an app?

No. The QR code opens your menu in the phone's browser. Guests type their name and order. That is all.

What do I need to start?

An internet connection and any phone, tablet or computer for your staff. Print the QR sheet, stick the codes on your tables, and you are ready. An 80 mm thermal printer is useful for KOTs and bills.

Can guests order a second round?

Yes. Each round goes to the kitchen as a new KOT on the same table order, and the final bill brings every round together.

Can someone order for a table they are not sitting at?

No. Every QR code carries a long secret code, not the table number, so nobody can guess another table's link. Switching a table off stops its code at once.

Do you take the payment from guests?

Not for now. Guests pay you the way they do today, by cash, card or UPI, and your cashier records how they paid. Split payments are fully supported.
